Text-to-Image Generative Modeling Without Language Pretraining

arXiv

Keshigeyan Chandrasegaran, Yue Zhao, Armin W. Thomas, Ehsan Adeli, Jiajun Wu, Juan Carlos Niebles, Li Fei-Fei, Michael Poli

AI Summary

This paper investigates whether internet-scale language pretraining is necessary for strong text-to-image (T2I) generation. It proposes Halcyon, a family of early-fusion multimodal autoregressive models trained entirely from scratch on a 100M image-text pair dataset. Halcyon models achieve competitive or superior T2I generation quality compared to larger, language-pretrained systems, using significantly less pretraining compute (up to 275x less). The research demonstrates that strong T2I systems can be developed without extensive language pretraining and can also acquire image captioning capabilities.

Reinforcement Learning with Visually Attributable On-Policy Self-Distillation

arXiv

Shiao Xie, Siyu Chen, Jianwei Lv, Xiandong Li, Yujin Wang

AI Summary

The core problem is "privileged-correction contamination" in on-policy self-distillation (OPSD), where token-level corrections from a privileged teacher may not be consistently attributable to visual evidence, leading to erroneous updates in multimodal reinforcement learning. The paper proposes Reinforcement Learning with Visually Attributable On-Policy Self-Distillation, an evidence-consistent paradigm instantiated by Visual-Attribution Gating (VAG). VAG validates privileged corrections by contrasting evidence-present and evidence-degraded teacher views, only admitting those directionally supported by visual evidence. This method consistently outperforms GRPO and OPSD, establishing new state-of-the-art average performance across six visual reasoning benchmarks.

AI Summary

Physical Words: A Vocabulary for Structuring Latent Reasoning in Robot Manipulation

arXiv

Jiaming Liu, Xinghao Zhu, Yiming Zhang, Shanghang Zhang, Yinxi Wang, Yichu Yang, Zhuoyang Liu, Dantong Niu, Soha Pouya, Juekun Li, Yan Chang

AI Summary

Robot manipulation reasoning struggles to combine high-level language semantics with structured, fine-grained physical dynamics for reusable action primitives. To bridge this, PhysWord is introduced, a reasoning-before-acting foundation model that uses "Physical Words" (atomic action verbs) as semantic anchors for continuous physical dynamics. It autoregressively predicts these words, aligning their hidden states with future observations to create a structured latent space for action generation. PhysWord achieves state-of-the-art success rates on UniVTAC (90.3%) and LIBERO-Long (99.6%), outperforms competitors on real-world tasks, and generalizes to unseen scenarios for long-horizon manipulation.

arXiv

AI Summary

Safety Mid-Training: Internalizing LLM Safety as a Foundational Capability

arXiv

Zhengyue Zhao, Liwei Jiang, Yejin Choi, Chaowei Xiao1

AI Summary

Current LLM safety alignment often degrades during subsequent training and incurs a trade-off with general capability because safety and general reasoning are treated as distinct training objectives. This paper proposes "safety mid-training," a stage between pre-training and post-training, which jointly develops safety and general reasoning by internalizing safety as a capability learned through a shared reasoning structure. This method significantly reduced attack success rates (72-81%) while *improving* general capability. The benefits persisted through subsequent general-only post-training stages, leading to a superior safety-utility frontier.

Fast Hubbard-Thouless Pumping with Local Counterdiabatic Driving

arXiv

Alexander Chansky

AI Summary

Counterdiabatic (CD) driving for fast protocols in many-body topological systems like Thouless pumping requires non-local terms, posing a challenge for implementation. Standard local approximations, while effective for non-interacting systems, fail to maintain quantized pumping in interacting models where interactions stabilize the topological phase. The authors propose a novel local CD ansatz that resolves nearest-neighbor currents by the interaction energy, with coefficients optimized via a ground-state-weighted action. This 14-coefficient ansatz successfully preserves quantized pumping in the interacting Rice-Mele model for extremely fast cycle times ($T=1/t$) with site-independent accuracy.

V-JEPA Hand: Simple, Accurate and Fast 3D Hand and Camera Trajectory Recovery from Egocentric Videos

arXiv

Ruicheng Li, Qixiu Li, Yu Deng, Sicheng Xu, Jiaolong Yang, Baining Guo

AI Summary

The core problem is accurately recovering metric 3D hand trajectories from egocentric videos, which requires jointly estimating hand motion and camera egomotion, a task often handled by complex multi-stage pipelines. V-JEPA Hand proposes a simple, end-to-end feed-forward model that jointly reconstructs bilateral hand motion and camera egomotion from egocentric clips. It utilizes a self-supervised V-JEPA 2.1 encoder for spatio-temporal features and a query-based predictor, trained end-to-end with mixed supervision. The model achieves state-of-the-art accuracy with the lowest MPJPE on multiple benchmarks, outperforms SLAM-based pipelines in trajectory error, and processes at 93.4 FPS.

HandsOn: A Large-Scale Simulation Benchmark for Generalizable Dexterous Manipulation

arXiv

Tianwei Luo, Yiming He, Hongjin Wu, Ting Pan, Debin Ren, Haorui Guo, Junlin Yang, Yuxin Zuo, Zhehao Kou, Kaisen Yang, Zhuoqun Lai, Yi Ding, Jiale Yu, Yuchen Zhang, Zijie Qiu, Haozhan Li, Ning Ding

AI Summary

The core problem is achieving general-purpose dexterous manipulation reliable across diverse embodiments, objects, and tasks. To address this, HandsOn is introduced as a composable simulation benchmark supporting multiple robotic arms and hands, ~200 assets, and 18,000 human demonstrations for 66 tasks, divided into Core-50 for learning and Transfer-16 for generalization. The best trained multi-task policy achieved 29.1% success on Core-50, while GPT-6 without benchmark finetuning attained 5.2% on Core-50 and 1.9% on Transfer-16, with analysis identifying failures in contact, action coordination, and goal maintenance.
