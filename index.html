arXiv

AI Summary

Long-Horizon Manipulation via Trace-Conditioned VLA Planning

arXiv

Isabella Liu, An-Chieh Cheng, Rui Yan, Geng Chen, Ri-Zhao Qiu, Xueyan Zou, Sha Yi, Hongxu Yin, Xiaolong Wang, Sifei Liu

AI Summary

Long-horizon manipulation for VLA policies is challenged by multi-step tasks and compounding execution errors. LoHo-Manip proposes a modular framework where a task-management VLM predicts a progress-aware remaining plan, combining a subtask sequence and a visual trace (2D keypoint trajectory). An executor VLA then conditions on and follows this trace, turning long-horizon decision-making into repeated local control. This receding-horizon replanning creates an implicit closed loop for automatic continuation and error recovery, demonstrating strong gains in long-horizon success, robustness, and out-of-distribution generalization on both simulated and real robots.

VistaBot: View-Robust Robot Manipulation via Spatiotemporal-Aware View Synthesis

arXiv

Songen Gu, Yuhang Zheng, Weize Li, Yupeng Zheng, Yating Feng, Xiang Li, Yilun Chen, Pengfei Li, Wenchao Ding

AI Summary

End-to-end robotic manipulation models suffer from limited robustness to camera viewpoint changes when trained with a fixed camera. VistaBot addresses this by integrating feed-forward geometric models with video diffusion models, using 4D geometry estimation, view synthesis latent extraction, and latent action learning, without requiring test-time camera calibration. Replay-buffer engineering for noise-robust quantum circuit optimization

arXiv

Akash Kundu, Sebastian Feld

AI Summary

Deep reinforcement learning for quantum circuit optimization faces challenges with inefficient replay buffers, costly curriculum-based architecture search, and discarded noiseless trajectories in noisy environments. This work introduces replay-buffer engineering solutions: ReaPER$+$ for annealed, reliability-aware sampling, OptCRLQAS to amortize expensive quantum-classical evaluations, and a lightweight replay-buffer transfer scheme to warm-start noisy learning with noiseless data. These methods achieve $4-32\times$ sample efficiency gains and discover more compact circuits, reduce wall-clock time per episode by up to $67.5\%$, and cut steps to chemical accuracy by $85-90\%$ in noisy tasks.

Unitary Time Evolution and Vacuum for a Quantum Stable Ghost

arXiv

Cédric Deffayet, Atabak Fathe Jalali, Aaron Held, Shinji Mukohyama, Alexander Vikman

AI Summary

The paper addresses the quantization of a classically stable system comprising a harmonic oscillator coupled to a ghost with negative kinetic energy, aiming to ensure quantum stability, unitary evolution, and a well-defined vacuum. Their method involves identifying an integral of motion with a positive discrete spectrum. This integral proves that the Hamiltonian has a pure point spectrum, evolution is unitary, a vacuum is well-defined, and expectation values of canonical variables are bounded, with numerical solutions confirming these findings.

arXiv

Yaxuan Li, Zhongyi Zhou, Yefei Chen, Yanjiang Guo, Jiaming Liu, Shanghang Zhang, Jianyu Chen, Yichen Zhu

AI Summary

Existing robot post-training is inefficient due to its reliance on costly real-world physical execution for human corrections. Hi-WM (Human-in-the-World-Model) proposes using a learned world model as a reusable corrective substrate; a human intervenes directly in simulated policy rollouts to provide targeted actions around failures, generating dense supervision for retraining. Hi-WM improved real-world success by 37.9 points over base policies and 19.0 points over baselines, demonstrating strong correlation between world-model evaluation and real-world performance (r=0.953).

Effects of Swarm Size Variability on Operator Workload

arXiv

William Hunt, Aleksandra Landowska, Horia A. Maior, Sarvapali D. Ramchurn, Mohammad Soorati

AI Summary

Dynamic swarm size variability in human-swarm teams presents a challenge by potentially causing operator overload and fluctuating workload. This paper investigated how the magnitude and direction of swarm size changes influence operator workload and performance using a simulated drone monitoring task. Results indicate that while objective performance remains largely stable, subjective workload is sensitive to change: small increases preserve lower workload, small decreases elevate workload (residue), and large changes in either direction attenuate these effects by inducing a cognitive reset. These findings provide guidance for managing swarm-size transitions.

Disorder-induced crossover from phase-averaging to mode-mixing regimes in magnetic domain walls of a second-order topological insulator

arXiv

Dong Zhou, Zhe Hou

AI Summary

The study investigates electronic transport across magnetic domain walls in 3D second-order topological insulators, specifically how Anderson disorder induces a crossover between transport regimes. Using theoretical frameworks for random-phase interference and inter-mode mixing, they found disorder suppresses Aharonov-Bohm oscillations, leading to a half-quantized conductance plateau. Crucially, conductance fluctuations exhibit a distinctive two-step plateau, identifying a crossover from a phase-averaging to a mode-mixing regime. This crossover is further characterized by changes in conductance distribution and Fano factor, highlighting the importance of second-order cumulants.

arXiv

AI Summary

This paper addresses active SLAM by recasting it as an optimal stochastic control problem under partial information, specifically formulating it as a nonstandard Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP).
