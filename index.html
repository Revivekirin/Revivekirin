Copy Less, Ground More: Overcoming Repetitive Copying in Long-Context Reasoning via Evidence-Aware Reinforcement Learning

arXiv

Lizhe Fang, Weizhou Shen, Tianyi Tang, Yisen Wang

AI Summary

Long-context LLMs suffer from "repetitive copying," where models extensively copy input text instead of productively solving problems, intensifying with context length and indicating insufficient grounding on key evidence. To address this, the authors propose GEAR (Grounding Evidence-Aware Reward), a reward shaping method that augments accuracy signals with a grounding reward for key evidence overlap and a penalty for irrelevant context overlap, supported by an automated evidence-annotation pipeline. GEAR consistently improves performance by up to +4.6 average points over standard RL, particularly in longer contexts, while also effectively reducing repetitive copying and thinking length.

Appearance Pointers -- Multimodal Region Control of Diffusion Transformers

arXiv

Rahul Sajnani, Yulia Gryaditskaya, Radomír Měch, Srinath Sridhar, Matheus Gadelha

AI Summary

Controllable image generation is challenged by the lack of precise regional control from text-only prompts and Diffusion Transformers' (DiTs) inability to spatially direct multimodal input tokens. This work introduces "appearance pointers," compact tokens generated by a region correspondence network and refined via spatial aggregation, which align text or image inputs with user-specified masks to provide spatial guidance to DiTs. This method creates the first modality-agnostic interface for localized multimodal control in DiTs without retraining the base model. It consistently matches or surpasses the performance of modality-specific state-of-the-art methods.

Masked Visual Actions for Unified World Modeling

arXiv

Hadi Alzayer, Wenlong Huang, Haonan Chen, Christopher Luey, Lvmin Zhang, Maneesh Agrawala, Gordon Wetzstein, Li Fei-Fei, Yilun Du, Jiajun Wu, Jia-Bin Huang

AI Summary

The core problem is effectively communicating physical actions to video-based world models in a visually aligned yet physically grounded manner. The proposed method, Masked Visual Actions, introduces a pixel-space control interface expressing actions as partially revealed trajectories of entities in a video. This unified model acts as both a forward dynamics predictor (from robot motion) and an inverse model (synthesizing robot motion for desired object motion). Finetuned with limited data, the model achieves high visual fidelity and controllability, improving downstream tasks like policy evaluation, model-based planning, and inverse modeling.

arXiv

Yuan Wang, Yongchao Du, Mengting Chen, Jinsong Lan, Xuetao Feng, Xiaoyong Zhu

AI Summary

ISO: An RLVR-Native Optimization Stack

arXiv

Hanqing Zhu, Wenyan Cong, Zhizhou Sha, Sagnik Mukherjee, Xinyuan Song, David González-Martínez, Xiaoxia Wu, Yuandong Tian, Shiwei Liu, David Z. Pan, Zhangyang "Atlas" Wang

AI Summary

The core problem is the poorly understood optimization layer that converts reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) feedback into language model weight updates. This work proposes Isospectral Optimization (ISO), an RLVR-native, fixed-spectrum framework based on "spectral inheritance," which reuses the base model's weight spectra while adapting behavior through changes in input and output singular frames. Online, ISO-Optimizer applies optimizers to frame variables, significantly improving training efficiency by reaching matched accuracy scores with substantially fewer steps across diverse reasoning and coding tasks.

arXiv

AI Summary

Off-Context GRPO: Learning to Reason on Hard Problems using Privileged Information

arXiv

Priyank Agrawal, Ankur Samanta, Shervin Ghasemlou, Jalaj Bhandari, Kavosh Asadi, Daniel Jiang, Aditya Modi

AI Summary

Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) fails on difficult problems because models receive zero learning signal when unable to generate any correct solutions. Off-Context GRPO (OC-GRPO) addresses this by utilizing privileged guidance, like solution prefixes, to generate "off-context" training rollouts. It then applies an importance-corrected objective to steer updates back toward the original unguided objective, preventing training destabilization. OC-GRPO achieves a 3.9\% absolute improvement (13.8\% relative gain) over vanilla GRPO on mathematical reasoning benchmarks with negligible cost.

AI Summary

Autonomous UAV flight in cluttered environments requires real-time onboard mapping and safe trajectory planning, conventionally separated and reliant on binary occupancy. Stochastic Multi-Objective Kinodynamic Planning Against Adversaries

arXiv

Thomas Marshall Vielmetti, Daniel Cherenson, Dimitra Panagou

AI Summary

This paper tackles multi-objective kinodynamic planning in stochastic adversarial environments, seeking Pareto-optimal paths that balance execution cost and safety risk, a problem where existing open-loop methods are overly conservative. The proposed method shifts the planning space to closed-loop policies, integrating sample-based risk evaluation via Monte-Carlo rollouts directly into tree construction, leading to Stochastic Multi-Objective RRT (SMO-RRT) and SMO-SST. SMO-RRT is proven probabilistically complete, and both algorithms derive finite-sample bounds on the probability of chance constraint violation for non-Gaussian uncertainty, enabling probabilistically safe planning.

AI Summary

Safe navigation in the delicate spinal subarachnoid space is challenged by conventional catheters generating harmful friction and shear. arXiv

Alessandro Scalese, Santhanakrishnan Narayanan, Constantinos Antoniou

AI Summary

Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for the Traffic Assignment Problem suffer from a spatial generalization gap due to transductive feature initializations tied to fixed network topologies, preventing transferability to new urban environments. This research proposes GUIDED, a network-agnostic initialization layer that injects travel demand as a scalar attribute on auxiliary virtual links, thereby standardizing the input space regardless of network scale. Additionally, it achieves an approximate 50% reduction in training time per epoch.

The Price of Reasoning: Cost-Quality Tradeoffs in Reinforcement Learning for Neural Machine Translation

arXiv

Michael Jungo, Aixiu An

AI Summary

The paper addresses whether the benefits of Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) in Neural Machine Translation (NMT) stem from induced reasoning or the general training paradigm, specifically the role of the reasoning trace. They investigate this by systematically omitting the reasoning trace during either training or inference phases. Key results show that including reasoning, particularly during inference, positively impacts translation quality. However, this comes at the cost of increased output tokens, revealing a significant cost-quality tradeoff.
