AI Summary

Carrollian quantum states and flat space holography

arXiv

Stefan Fredenhagen, Stefan Prohazka, Robert Tiefenbacher

AI Summary

This paper algebraically investigates free Carrollian quantum field theories (QFTs) to understand their states and implications for flat space holography. It constructs Carrollian Weyl algebras, finding regular vacuum and thermal states for massive theories, but identifying challenges in defining regular distinguished vacuums for massless theories. A key result is the construction of a specific quasifree state relevant for Carrollian holography, where the Hilbert space factorizes, emphasizing the critical role of infrared degrees of freedom in the boundary theory.

ATRS: Adaptive Trajectory Re-splitting via a Shared Neural Policy for Parallel Optimization

arXiv

Jiajun Yu, Guodong Liu, Li Wang, Pengxiang Zhou, Wentao Liu, Yin He, Chao Xu, Fei Gao, Yanjun Cao

AI Summary

Parallel trajectory optimization via ADMM suffers from stagnation and slow convergence due to fixed, predefined problem decomposition and lagging subproblems in constrained regions. ATRS addresses this by embedding a shared Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) policy into the ADMM loop, which adaptively re-splits stagnating trajectory segments online. This DRL approach, modeled as a Multi-Agent Shared-Policy MDP, uses internal solver states for zero-shot generalization and selects segments via a confidence-based mechanism. ATRS significantly accelerates convergence, reducing iterations by up to 26.0% and computation time by up to 19.1%, with confirmed real-world applicability for both offline and real-time planning.

Thinking Without Words: Efficient Latent Reasoning with Abstract Chain-of-Thought

arXiv

Keshav Ramji, Tahira Naseem, Ramón Fernandez Astudillo

AI Summary

Explicit verbal chains-of-thought (CoT) are effective but generate costly, long sequences, while existing non-verbal methods lack performance. This paper proposes Abstract Chain-of-Thought (Abstract-CoT), a post-training mechanism where language models generate a short sequence of discrete "abstract" tokens from a reserved vocabulary instead of natural language CoT. The method involves a warm-up phase (bottlenecking verbal CoT, self-distillation) followed by reinforcement learning for optimization. Abstract-CoT achieves comparable performance to verbal CoT across diverse reasoning tasks with up to 11.6x fewer reasoning tokens, generalizes across models, and reveals an emergent power law distribution in its abstract vocabulary.

Dense Matter and Compact Stars in Strong Magnetic Fields

arXiv

Monika Sinha, Vivek Baruah Thapa

AI Summary

Extreme magnetic fields ($10^{17}-10^{18}$ G) in magnetars are expected to profoundly alter the microscopic and macroscopic properties of dense matter in compact stars. This review examines how strong magnetic fields affect fermionic matter via Landau quantization and anomalous magnetic moment interactions. It further discusses magnetized hadronic matter using relativistic mean-field approaches, considering emergent degrees of freedom like hyperons, meson condensates, and quark matter. The consequences of these effects for neutron-star structure and observational constraints are outlined.

BERAG: Bayesian Ensemble Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-based Visual Question Answering

arXiv

Jinghong Chen, Jingbiao Mei, Guangyu Yang, Bill Byrne

AI Summary

Standard RAG's document concatenation causes "lost-in-the-middle," attribution issues, and poor scaling, particularly problematic in knowledge-based Visual Question Answering with long, imperfect contexts. BERAG proposes conditioning language models on individual retrieved documents, updating document posterior probabilities as ensemble weights token-by-token using Bayes' rule during generation. This method achieves substantial improvements over standard RAG in VQA tasks and multimodal "needle-in-a-haystack" benchmarks. Key results include mitigating the "lost-in-the-middle" effect, enabling probabilistic re-ranking, clear attribution, faster decoding via document pruning, and detection of insufficient grounding.

arXiv

AI Summary

This paper addresses the online multi-objective co-design problem for compositional systems, aiming to identify non-dominated trade-offs efficiently despite expensive evaluations. RedVLA: Physical Red Teaming for Vision-Language-Action Models

arXiv

Yuhao Zhang, Borong Zhang, Jiaming Fan, Jiachen Shen, Yishuai Cai, Yaodong Yang, Jiaming Ji

AI Summary

RedVLA, a novel physical red teaming framework, is proposed to address this via a two-stage process: Risk Scenario Synthesis creates task-feasible initial risk scenes by positioning risk factors in critical interaction regions, followed by Risk Amplification which refines risk factor states through gradient-free optimization to stably elicit unsafe behaviors. Data-Free Contribution Estimation in Federated Learning using Gradient von Neumann Entropy

CVPR

Asim Ukaye, Mubarak Abdu-Aguye, Nurbek Tastan, Karthik Nandakumar

AI Summary

Existing Federated Learning client contribution estimation methods risk privacy or manipulation by relying on validation data or self-reported information. This paper proposes a data-free signal based on the matrix von Neumann entropy of final-layer updates, measuring information diversity. Two schemes are introduced: SpectralFed, which uses entropy as aggregation weights, and SpectralFuse, which fuses entropy with class-specific alignment via a Kalman filter. Entropy-derived scores consistently show a high correlation with client accuracy across various non-IID benchmarks, serving as an effective, data-free indicator of client contribution.
