Modality-Autoregressive World-Action Models

arXiv

Adam Hung, Bardienus P. Duisterhof, Deva Ramanan, Jeffrey Ichnowski

AI Summary

The core problem addresses how to best combine diverse visual modalities (depth, features, point tracks) within World-Action Models (WAMs), as existing models primarily rely on RGB predictions. The proposed method, ModAR, is the first WAM to autoregressively denoise and predict multiple future modalities sequentially, allowing subsequent predictions to condition on previously generated information before action prediction. Key results show ModAR's sequential generation outperforms other WAM formulations, achieving a 75% average success rate with 20x fewer training FLOPs and no pretraining compared to a strong baseline, and benefits from predicting modalities like point tracks, DINO features, and depth, while RGB did not provide consistent benefit.

ScienceBuddy introduces an interactive scientific research workspace to integrate continually improving scientific agents into researchers' workflows, learning from their requests, feedback, and execution. LACE: Layer-Wise Compression for Dynamic Frame Rate Codecs

arXiv

Thanapat Trachu, Samuele Cornell, William Chen, Shinji Watanabe

AI Summary

Neural audio codecs incur high computational costs due to their high frame rates, and current dynamic frame rate codecs inefficiently apply a single compression step across all quantization layers, forcing shared segmentation despite varying residual embedding rates. LACE (Layer-Adaptive Codec Encoding) proposes applying an independent compression step at each quantization layer to enable layer-specific segmentation boundaries. It further introduces union alignment and boundary anchor mechanisms to ensure consistent token durations for downstream text-to-speech (TTS) applications. LACE achieves a better rate-quality trade-off in reconstruction and improves TTS inference efficiency while maintaining competitive synthesis quality.

ENCP: Episode-Normalized Conformal Prediction for Vision-and-Language Navigation

arXiv

Vicky Feliren, A. Taufiq Asyhari, Muhamad Risqi U. Saputra

AI Summary

Standard conformal prediction fails to provide reliable coverage guarantees for the dependent, variable-length steps within Vision-Language Navigation (VLN) episodes. To address this, the paper proposes Episode-Normalized Conformal Prediction (ENCP), which rescales nonconformity scores by a policy's residual confidence and calibrates a single maximum score per episode. ENCP theoretically guarantees step-wise coverage with a given probability and empirically meets all reported step-coverage targets across various VLN policies and datasets (R2R, REVERIE).

Rotational Brownian Motion and $\mathrm{J/ψ}$ Spin Alignment in Heavy-Ion Collisions

arXiv

Bhagyarathi Sahoo, Captain R. Singh

AI Summary

This study investigates J/ψ spin alignment in heavy-ion collisions, which probes deconfined QCD characteristics. The proposed method models the rotational Brownian motion of charm quarks in a strong magnetic field, deriving an analytical expression for $c$-$\bar{c}$ pair polarization via the Fokker-Planck equation with spin-vorticity coupling. These polarized pairs then form J/ψ, allowing calculation of the spin-density matrix element $ρ_{00}$. Key results include a microscopic description of heavy-quark spin transport and the suggestion that rotational diffusion is a possible mechanism for observed J/ψ spin alignment.

Dissecting Motion-Prior Regularization for Data-Scarce Robotic Insertion

arXiv

Ning Hu, Shuai Li, Jindong Tan

AI Summary

JustFit: 200K-Token LLM Serving on a 24 GiB Laptop with Just-in-Time State Management

arXiv

Yuhua Chen

AI Summary

Local serving of large LLMs with extensive context and execution states strains laptop memory. JustFit, an MLX-based inference runtime, addresses this by combining KVExec for compressed KV cache, PhaseSwap for component residency, and StateTrans for state-preserving transitions, enabling just-in-time state materialization and release. On a 24 GiB laptop, JustFit increased single-request context capacity to 212,992 positions (6.93x baseline), completing over 200K tokens. It demonstrated efficient performance and enabled complex reasoning by correctly solving 29 of 30 AIME problems within a 16 GiB peak memory footprint.

Gaussian Processes for Modelling Spatial Fields with Robot Swarms

arXiv

Guillermo Legarda Herranz, Gianpiero Francesca, Mauro Birattari

AI Summary

Robot swarms face the core problem of modeling spatial fields without external positioning systems, as current methods rely on self-localization. The proposed solution is Location-Unaware Gaussian Process Regression (LU-GPR), which enables robots to infer field properties and agree on a common frame of reference using only local sensing and communication, integrating an online algorithm with a product of experts model. Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability for Hybrid Systems: Unified Goal-Driven Control with Safety Guarantees

arXiv

Javier Borquez, Shuang Peng, Somil Bansal

AI Summary

Ensuring safety and performance in hybrid dynamical systems, particularly contact-rich robotics, is challenging due to the intricate coupling of continuous and discrete dynamics. This work extends Hamilton-Jacobi (HJ) reachability analysis to hybrid systems, defining safe sets via a generalized value function and providing a numerical computation algorithm. It introduces a hybrid least-restrictive safety filter and novel hybrid backward reach-avoid tubes to integrate performance objectives, enabling the synthesis of control policies that guarantee both safety and task completion. The framework's effectiveness is validated through simulations and real-world quadrupedal robot experiments.

SlotDiT: Object-Centric Representations for Diffusion Transformers

arXiv

Gjergj Plepi, Sven Behnke

AI Summary

Existing latent diffusion models for video generation and robotics lack explicit object-centric semantic structure in their pixel-level or VAE-based latent representations. SlotDiT introduces a text-guided Diffusion Transformer operating in a slot-based object-centric latent space, decomposing scenes into individual entity slots and autoregressively denoising future slot trajectories based on language instructions. This method achieves competitive video generation quality, consistently improves task-completion rates across four robotic datasets, and offers computational efficiency, demonstrating the power of object-centric structure as an inductive bias.

arXiv

Linan Deng, Xing Liu, Lin Hong, Feng Hua, Guijun Ma, Zuogong Yue, Fumin Zhang

AI Summary

arXiv

Exact Fusion and Coordinated Exploration in Multi-Robot Active Inference

arXiv

Peng Wu, Mohsen Imani, Amidu Kamara, Md Tamzeed Islam, Seyede Fatemeh Ghoreishi, Mahdi Imani

AI Summary

Multi-robot active inference teams face two core issues: overcounting the common prior during belief fusion and redundant exploration due to independent planning. The proposed method introduces "evidence increments" to the shared natural parameter, using realized increments for exact fusion and expected increments for coordinated exploration via conditional gain and sequential commitment. Key results show fusion correction resolves the prior issue, anticipated evidence removes exploration redundancy, and sequential commitment recovers most of the value of centralized joint planning at linear cost.

Exact solutions of nonreciprocal Su-Schrieffer-Heeger model with domain walls

arXiv

Tong Wang, Mengjie Yang, Flore K. Kunst

AI Summary

This work addresses the lack of exact analytical solutions for domain-wall physics in non-Hermitian systems subject to the skin effect by deriving closed-form quantization conditions for nonreciprocal Su-Schrieffer-Heeger chains with inverted-coupling interfaces. Using symmetry-based ansatzes, the authors reveal that domain walls induce novel finite-energy localized states that detach from the bulk continuum alongside standard skin and topological zero modes. Furthermore, generalizing this framework to a two-dimensional Lieb lattice reveals that competing nonreciprocities enable tunable wave funneling toward codimension-one and codimension-two boundaries.

arXiv

Gysella Imrell, Emanuele Miotto, Mahya Mohammadi Kashani, Mauro Conti, Alberto Giaretta
