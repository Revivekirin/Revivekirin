AI Summary

Video foundation models frequently suffer from geometric inconsistencies, and existing 3D prior injection methods are computationally expensive and unscalable. World-R1 proposes a reinforcement learning framework, utilizing Flow-GRPO with feedback from pre-trained 3D and vision-language models on a specialized text dataset, alongside a periodic decoupled training strategy, to enforce 3D constraints without architectural modifications. Tuna-2: Pixel Embeddings Beat Vision Encoders for Multimodal Understanding and Generation

arXiv

Zhiheng Liu, Weiming Ren, Xiaoke Huang, Shoufa Chen, Tianhong Li, Mengzhao Chen, Yatai Ji, Sen He, Jonas Schult, Belinda Zeng, Tao Xiang, Wenhu Chen, Ping Luo, Luke Zettlemoyer, Yuren Cong

AI Summary

Existing unified multimodal models face misalignment and non-end-to-end optimization due to their reliance on separate pretrained vision encoders for understanding and generation. Tuna-2 proposes a native unified multimodal model that directly uses simple pixel/patch embeddings for both visual understanding and generation, entirely discarding traditional vision encoders. Tuna-2 achieves state-of-the-art performance in multimodal benchmarks, matching latent-space approaches for high-quality image generation. Its encoder-free design also demonstrates stronger multimodal understanding at scale, particularly for fine-grained perception, indicating that pretrained vision encoders are not essential for scalable visual representation learning.

CAbLECAR: efficiently scheduling QLDPC codes on a tileable spin qubit chip with shuttling

arXiv

Jason D. Chadwick, Frederic T. Chong

AI Summary

Implementing QLDPC codes, which require nonlocal connectivity, is challenging for semiconductor spin qubits. This work proposes CAbLECAR, a coordinated shuttle scheduling algorithm for arbitrary QLDPC codes on a tileable, shuttling-based spin qubit architecture, tailored for shuttling noise. This approach extends the feasible shuttling range by 5-10x and yields schedules up to 86% faster than hand-optimized ones. Specific QLDPC codes implemented with CAbLECAR significantly improve encoding efficiency and reduce logical error rates by orders of magnitude compared to prior surface code implementations.

arXiv

AI Summary

To address this, the paper introduces the Visual Degraded Control Suite (VDCS) benchmark and proposes Agent-Centric Observations with Mixture-of-Experts (ACO-MoE). arXiv

Lixian Chen, Mingxuan Huang, Yanhui Chen, Junyi Lin, Yang Shi

AI Summary

Asymmetric modality shifts in vision-language models cause standard entropy-based test-time adaptation to fail, leading to increased error when unreliable modalities dominate fusion. The proposed MG-MTTA method addresses this by formulating adaptation as a majorization-guided de-mixing problem, updating a lightweight gate/adapter with an objective that combines fused-posterior entropy minimization and a reliability-aware gate prior. arXiv
