Don't Drop the BATON: Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation via Agentic Subtask Exploration and Transition-aware Memory

arXiv

Bingxin Xu, Yuzhang Shang, Emilio Ferrara

AI Summary

Long-horizon robot manipulation suffers from compounded errors, prohibitive exploration costs for multi-stage tasks, and a lack of explicit transition management between subtasks. BATON addresses these by making the subtask the unit of exploration, reducing cost from multiplicative to additive and attributing failures to single stages. It also employs transition-aware memory with a verifier agent, handoff, and lookahead transitions to ensure subtask compatibility. This method improves task success by 11.6% and cumulative success by 14.9% on the RoboMemArena benchmark.

Q-based Variational Inverse Reinforcement Learning

arXiv

Ondrej Bajgar, Peter Tisnikar, Alessandro Abate, Konstantinos Gatsis, Maike Osborne

AI Summary

Explicitly specifying human preferences for AI is often infeasible, a problem addressed by inferring reward functions from expert behavior. This paper introduces Q-based Variational IRL (QVIRL), a novel Bayesian IRL method that recovers a posterior distribution over rewards by learning a variational distribution over optimal Q-values. QVIRL combines scalability with uncertainty quantification, demonstrating strong apprenticeship learning performance across diverse tasks and being the first Bayesian IRL method to train from raw pixel observations.

arXiv

AI Summary

arXiv

AI Summary

Existing planning for multi-segment suction-based soft robots heavily relies on manually designed, environment-specific gaits. Security of Foundation-Model-Powered Embodied Agents: Attack Surfaces, Attacks, Defenses, and Evaluation

arXiv

Jiawei Liu, Jiacheng Guo, Tian Zhang, Yiwei Xu, Juan Wang, Jinlin Fan, Bowen Xiao

AI Summary

Foundation models powering embodied agents create significant security risks, where digital inputs can lead to malicious physical actions, and existing threat classifications fail to identify adversary entry points within the control loop. This paper proposes a novel trust-boundary-centric security survey, separating attack surfaces from mechanisms across five layers and twelve distinct system points based on the first-compromised-trust-boundary. Analysis shows attack research focuses on multimodal perception and action interfaces, with defenses concentrating on action-level runtime protection, leaving areas like multi-agent trust and long-term memory comparatively underexplored. The work highlights open challenges in state provenance, compositional defenses, and unified closed-loop evaluation.

AI Summary

Generalist VLAs struggle with high-dimensional, interdependent humanoid whole-body loco-manipulation, and efficiently refining these models online is challenging due to their large backbones. Policy Iteration with Human Feedback: Bringing Post-Training RL to In-context Learning

arXiv

Minh-Ha Nguyen, Cathy Shyr

AI Summary

The core problem is enhancing the adaptive performance of fixed-weight language models for complex in-context learning tasks by systematically addressing recurrent failures. The proposed method, Policy Iteration with Human Feedback (PIHF), utilizes a pretrained LLM as an execution substrate, employing a versioned natural-language policy and tool set. A language-model critic and human expert collaboratively review task trajectories to localize failures and generate revisions, with the expert retaining final authority over policy updates. Key results show PIHF significantly improved Recall@1 in ultra-rare-disease benchmarks, achieving gains of 32.7 percentage points for GPT-5.4 and 31.1 points for Qwen3.6-35B across diverse LLM executors.

Adaptive Repulsive Pheromone Clustering for Foraging Robot Swarms

arXiv

Carlos Pena-Caballero, Constantine Tarawneh, Qi Lu

AI Summary

The Central Place Foraging Algorithm (CPFA) often re-explores previously visited regions, decreasing efficiency as resources dwindle. This paper proposes Adaptive Repulsive Pheromone Clustering (ARPC), where robots deposit repulsive pheromones to mark explored locations. These waypoints are clustered near the nest to guide robots away from low-value search areas toward unvisited regions. ARPC consistently outperforms CPFA and Grid-Based CPFA in simulations, yielding significant gains in resource discovery efficiency during early (10%) and late-stage (up to 60%) collection.

AI Summary

Reinforcement learning (RL) through complex agent harnesses on long-horizon tasks is challenging and largely unexplored due to scalability issues. This work presents ClawGym II, a unified black-box RL framework that decouples policy optimization from opaque harness execution using sandbox-based infrastructure for concurrent rollouts. TRACE-Bench: Decomposing and Diagnosing Multi-Reference Image Generation

arXiv

Haoran Wang, Chaofan Ma, Ran Yi, Lizhuang Ma

AI Summary

Existing benchmarks for multi-reference image generation suffer from fragmented coverage and poor diagnostic value due to their task-oriented organization. The authors propose a capability-oriented perspective, formalizing multi-reference prompts as compositional formulas over four atomic operators: Anchor, Disentangle, Apply, and Compose. Based on this, they construct TRACE-Bench, enabling operator-aligned evaluation and diagnostic failure localization for these capabilities. Evaluation of leading models reveals that the primary bottlenecks lie in disentanglement and attribute binding, rather than scene-level composition, with even the best model achieving only 0.74 attribute fidelity.

PersonaShot: Benchmarking Person-Centric Narrative Continuity in Multi-Shot Video Generation

arXiv

Yuji Wang, Yuheng Chen, Teng Hu, Ran Yi, Yijia Hong, Han Feng, Weijian Cao, Chengjie Wang, Lizhuang Ma, Jiangning Zhang

AI Summary

Existing multi-shot video generation benchmarks lack robust evaluation of person-centric narrative continuity, failing to measure physical and emotional coherence across cuts with criterion-specific methods. To address this, PersonaShot is introduced as the first benchmark for person-centric narrative continuity, comprising ~1,000 multi-shot segments and 16 metrics spanning physical continuity, affective dynamics, and cinematic grammar, assessed by human-aligned, specialist AI evaluators. Evaluation reveals a significant gap between perceptual quality and cross-shot narrative continuity in state-of-the-art models, showing frequent physical-state resets, abrupt affective shifts, and broken cinematic relations, with evaluators strongly agreeing with human judgments.
