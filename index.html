Can Learning to Answer Spatial Questions Improve Novel-View Generation?

arXiv

Jahyeok Koo, Hyeonseo Yu, Seungho Jang, Jaewoo Jung, Jiwon Kang, Seokju Cho, Seungryong Kim

AI Summary

Novel-view generation from limited observations struggles with explicitly modeling high-level semantic and spatial properties beyond fine-grained geometry. This work proposes Ask4NV, a model built on a pretrained multimodal Mixture-of-Transformers architecture, which incorporates camera geometry and leverages 3D language understanding through joint self-attention with modality-aware masking to provide complementary supervision. Ask4NV achieves strong image quality and geometric consistency in novel-view generation, competitive 3D language understanding, and improved cross-view correspondence and 3D-aware feature space similarity.

SafeDriveVLA: Navigation-Conditioned World Model Dreaming for Conflict-Aware End-to-End Autonomous Driving

arXiv

Shaoyuan Xie, Zihan Zhang, Jingxuan Wang, Jiashu Qu, Xiaoqing Liang, Lingdong Kong, Junchi Lu, Henrik I. Christensen, Qi Alfred Chen

AI Summary

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) autonomous driving models are dangerously vulnerable to conflicting, unsafe, or adversarial natural language instructions from users. To resolve this, the authors introduce SafeDriveVLA, which decouples conflict reasoning from action generation using a three-level compliance mode guided by a navigation-conditioned world model that simulates the safety of following commands. On Bench2Drive, SafeDriveVLA achieves an 88.16 Driving Score and a 68.64% Success Rate, while boosting closed-loop language following by 49.3% and significantly reducing collisions under unsafe instructions compared to prior baselines.
