WorldSculpt: Generating Compositional Worlds from Grounded Videos

arXiv

Muyao Niu, Jixuan He, Ruihan Yu, Lian Fu, Yonghao Yu, Zheng-Hui Huang, Yifan Zhan, Fengbo Lan, Yongtao Ge, Yinqiang Zheng, Kaipeng Zhang, Zhixiang Wang

AI Summary

The core problem is generating compositional 3D representations of densely cluttered scenes with hundreds of objects, challenged by severe occlusion and incomplete geometry. WorldSculpt proposes adapting a strong single-object 3D generative prior (instantiated with Pixal3D) by adding a multi-view conditioning pathway to ground object generation in multiple posed observations. Crucially, the model is finetuned only on single objects but generalizes to complex scenes without scene-level training. It consistently outperforms prior approaches on a new photorealistic benchmark (UE-MeshyScene), showing larger gains with increased scene complexity and occlusion, and can convert 3DGS worlds into compositional mesh scenes.

Adaptation Needs in Robotic Systems: Assessing Behavior Trees and Their Enhancement

arXiv

Mehran Rostamnia, Gianluca Filippone, Ricardo Caldas, Patrizio Pelliccione

AI Summary

The core problem is assessing if classical Behavior Trees (BTs) adequately meet the adaptation needs of robotic systems operating in dynamic, uncertain environments. The paper conducts a literature-driven study, classifying robotic adaptation needs and analyzing the capabilities and limitations of both classical and enhanced BT approaches. Key results show classical BTs are insufficient for complex adaptation (e.g., runtime restructuring, learning), while enhanced BTs address some limitations but often introduce new ones. This analysis guides when enhanced BTs are necessary and identifies remaining challenges for adaptive robotic control architectures.
