123D: Unifying Multi-Modal Autonomous Driving Data at Scale

arXiv

Daniel Dauner, Valentin Charraut, Bastian Berle, Tianyu Li, Long Nguyen, Jiabao Wang, Changhui Jing, Maximilian Igl, Holger Caesar, Boris Ivanovic, Yiyi Liao, Andreas Geiger, Kashyap Chitta

AI Summary

Autonomous driving datasets are fragmented with diverse modalities, formats, and inconsistent annotations, making large-scale, unified analysis and cross-dataset generalization challenging. The 123D framework addresses this by providing a single API to unify multi-modal data, storing each modality as an independent timestamped event stream for flexible access. Using 123D, the authors consolidated eight real-world and one synthetic dataset, conducted systematic studies on annotation and calibration, and demonstrated applications like cross-dataset 3D object detection transfer and reinforcement learning for planning.

GRAPHLCP: Structure-Aware Localized Conformal Prediction on Graphs

arXiv

Peyman Baghershahi, Fangxin Wang, Debmalya Mandal, Sourav Medya

AI Summary

Applying Conformal Prediction (CP) to Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) is challenging due to the combinatorial nature of graphs, resulting in uncertain predictions and unreliable, inefficient localization from existing embedding-space proximity methods. GRAPHLCP is proposed, a localized CP framework that explicitly incorporates graph topology and inter-node dependencies. It uses a feature-aware densification step and a Personalized PageRank-based kernel to model structural proximity for topology-dependent anchor sampling and weighting. This approach guarantees marginal coverage with finite samples while efficiently achieving favorable test conditional coverage across various scenarios.

Flow-OPD: On-Policy Distillation for Flow Matching Models

arXiv

Zhen Fang, Wenxuan Huang, Yu Zeng, Yiming Zhao, Shuang Chen, Kaituo Feng, Yunlong Lin, Lin Chen, Zehui Chen, Shaosheng Cao, Feng Zhao

AI Summary

Flow Matching (FM) text-to-image models struggle with multi-task alignment due to reward sparsity, gradient interference, and a 'seesaw effect' of competing metrics. Flow-OPD is introduced as a novel post-training framework that integrates on-policy distillation into FM models. It uses a two-stage approach: training domain-specialized teachers, then consolidating their expertise into a student via on-policy sampling, dense trajectory-level supervision, and Manifold Anchor Regularization (MAR) to preserve aesthetic quality. Flow-OPD significantly improves GenEval (63 to 92) and OCR accuracy (59 to 94) on Stable Diffusion 3.5 Medium, outperforming vanilla GRPO by roughly 10 points while maintaining fidelity and demonstrating a "teacher-surpassing" effect.

Reinforcement Learning for Exponential Utility: Algorithms and Convergence in Discounted MDPs

arXiv

Gugan Thoppe, L. A. Prashanth, Ankur Naskar, Sanjay Bhat

AI Summary

Reinforcement learning for exponential-utility optimization in discounted MDPs lacks principled value-based algorithms for fixed risk-aversion. This paper addresses the gap by deriving two Q-value-style extensions based on a Bellman-type equation, demonstrating their operators are contractions and that they induce optimal greedy policies. This leads to two model-free algorithms: a two-timescale Q-learning-style algorithm with established almost-sure convergence and finite-time rates, and a one-timescale algorithm whose convergence is proven through advanced analytical techniques. This work provides a foundational framework for value-based RL under exponential-utility objectives.

Beyond Pairs: Your Language Model is Secretly Optimizing a Preference Graph

ACL

Ning Liu, Chuanneng Sun, Kristina Klinkner, Shervin Malmasi

AI Summary

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) struggles with rich, multi-rollout preference data by collapsing it into pairs, discarding transitivity, and leading to unstable optimization. GraphDPO addresses this by operating on directed acyclic preference graphs induced by rollout rankings, optimizing a Plackett--Luce-inspired objective that aggregates supervision over graph neighborhoods to enforce transitivity and supports ground-truth anchoring. Despite maintaining linear per-prompt complexity, experiments on reasoning and program synthesis tasks demonstrate GraphDPO's superior performance, establishing it as a scalable and robust alternative.

SphereVAD: Training-Free Video Anomaly Detection via Geodesic Inference on the Unit Hypersphere

arXiv

Chao Huang, Penfei Wei, Wei Wang, Jie Wen, Zhihua Wang, Li Shen, Wenqi Ren, Xiaochun Cao

AI Summary

Existing video anomaly detection (VAD) methods struggle with rapid deployment due to their dependency on large-scale annotations and extensive task-specific training. SphereVAD addresses this by proposing a fully training-free, zero-shot framework that leverages intermediate-layer features from pre-trained multimodal large language models. It recasts anomaly discrimination as von Mises-Fisher likelihood-ratio geodesic inference on a unit hypersphere, employing Frechet mean centering, Holistic Scene Attention, and Spherical Geodesic Pulling for principled geometric reasoning. SphereVAD achieves state-of-the-art results among training-free approaches on three major benchmarks and remains competitive with fully supervised baselines.

arXiv

AI Summary

Existing feedback-based LLM self-improvement systems face limitations, requiring offline setups, privileged ground-truth contexts, and lacking efficiency for online federated learning on resource-constrained edge devices. To address this, SPEAR (Self-Play Enhancement via Advantage-Weighted Refinement) introduces an efficient online learning algorithm for federated LLM fine-tuning. arXiv

Hanlin Cai, Kai Li, Houtianfu Wang, Haofan Dong, Yichen Li, Falko Dressler, Ozgur B. Akan

AI Summary

Federated fine-tuning (FFT) of large language models (LLMs), while privacy-preserving, is vulnerable to adversarial model manipulation where malicious participants upload corrupted updates. This paper proposes AugMP, an augmented model manipulation strategy that uses a graph representation learning framework to guide the generation of malicious updates. An iterative algorithm, based on an augmented Lagrangian dual formulation, optimizes these updates to embed adversarial objectives while preserving benign-like parameter characteristics for stealth. AugMP achieves strong manipulation, reducing global LLM accuracy by up to 26% and local agent accuracy by 22%, while evading conventional defenses due to its high statistical and geometric consistency with benign updates.

Noise-Driven Free Boundaries In The Compressible Navier-Stokes Equations

arXiv

Gianmarco Del Sarto, Matthias Hieber, Tarek Zöchling

AI Summary

This paper investigates a stochastic free-boundary problem for the 3D barotropic compressible Navier-Stokes equations, where noise from a Stratonovich stochastic flow drives the free boundary's evolution and an Itô forcing is included. The authors transform the problem using a stochastic Lagrangian map, representing density via the flow's Jacobian. They solve the transformed system by combining stochastic maximal regularity, deterministic estimates, and a localized contraction argument, achieving local pathwise well-posedness up to a positive stopping time, with strictly positive density and pathwise uniqueness.

Energy-Resolved Quantum Geometry from Středa Response: Driven-Dissipative Bosonic Lattices and Disordered Systems

arXiv

Anaïs Defossez, Baptiste Bermond, Lucila Peralta Gavensky, Nathan Goldman

AI Summary

The core problem is to directly access the energy-resolved Středa marker, which reveals detailed quantum geometry of Bloch bands, particularly for understanding phenomena like topological Anderson insulators. This paper proposes using driven-dissipative bosonic lattices with controlled uniform-strength random-phase pumping and uniform loss, which functions as a Lorentzian filter for eigenmode occupations. This method provides direct access to integrated and coarse-grained energy-resolved Středa responses, making these platforms versatile probes for quantum geometry and successfully elucidating the quantum-geometric structure of topological Anderson insulators.
