Multi-Robot Coordination in V2X Environments

arXiv

John Pravin Arockiasamy, Alexey Vinel

AI Summary

The paper addresses decentralized cooperation among social robots in complex V2X urban traffic environments, including integrating vulnerable road users (VRUs). It proposes a V2X communication framework with new robot-centric facility-layer services: the Robot Awareness Service (RAS) for role-aware awareness and VRU integration, and the Robot Maneuver Coordination Service (RMCS) for event-driven, low-latency coordination without centralized infrastructure. A real-world proof of concept demonstrated deterministic multi-robot coordination assisting a pedestrian, and simulations showed RAS integrates non-V2X VRUs in safety-critical areas while reducing redundant V2X VRU transmissions, thereby lowering channel load.

Beyond Negative Rollouts: Positive-Only Policy Optimization with Implicit Negative Gradients

arXiv

Mingwei Xu, Hao Fang

AI Summary

Current RLVR methods like GRPO struggle with negative rollouts due to a lack of failure gradation and sparse binary rewards, making it hard to extract meaningful reward signals. This paper proposes Positive-Only Policy Optimization (POPO), an RLVR framework that learns exclusively from online positive rollouts. POPO uses bounded importance sampling over positive rollouts to generate implicit negative gradients via rollout redistribution and stabilizes optimization with a siamese policy network and a bounded similarity penalty. Experiments show POPO achieves comparable or superior performance to GRPO, such as 36.67% on AIME 2025, surpassing GRPO's 30.00%.

Are We Making Progress in Multimodal Domain Generalization? A Comprehensive Benchmark Study

arXiv

Hao Dong, Hongzhao Li, Shupan Li, Muhammad Haris Khan, Eleni Chatzi, Olga Fink

AI Summary

Progress in Multimodal Domain Generalization (MMDG) is obscured by fragmented research, inconsistent evaluations, and a lack of assessment for real-world challenges like corruptions or missing modalities. To address this, the authors introduce MMDG-Bench, a unified benchmark standardizing evaluation across six datasets, diverse tasks, modality combinations, and methods, while also assessing robustness, missing-modality generalization, and trustworthiness. Key findings reveal that specialized MMDG methods offer only marginal gains over baselines, no method consistently excels, a significant performance gap persists, and all evaluated methods degrade severely under corruptions and missing modalities, often compromising trustworthiness.

StraTA: Incentivizing Agentic Reinforcement Learning with Strategic Trajectory Abstraction

arXiv

Xiangyuan Xue, Yifan Zhou, Zidong Wang, Shengji Tang, Philip Torr, Wanli Ouyang, Lei Bai, Zhenfei Yin

AI Summary

Large language models struggle with long-horizon decision-making as reactive methods hinder exploration and credit assignment. StraTA (Strategic Trajectory Abstraction) introduces an explicit trajectory-level strategy, sampling a compact plan from the initial state and conditioning subsequent actions on it. The framework jointly trains strategy generation and action execution via a hierarchical GRPO-style rollout, enhanced by diverse strategy exploration and critical self-judgment. Experiments on ALFWorld, WebShop, and SciWorld show StraTA consistently improves sample efficiency and performance, reaching 93.1% and 84.2% success rates, respectively, and outperforming frontier models on SciWorld.

arXiv

AI Summary

FedAttr: Towards Privacy-preserving Client-Level Attribution in Federated LLM Fine-tuning

arXiv

Su Zhang, Junfeng Guo, Heng Huang

AI Summary

Watermark detection methods for centralized LLM fine-tuning struggle to attribute watermarked data to specific clients in federated learning (FL) due to secure aggregation's privacy guarantees. FedAttr is proposed as a client-level attribution protocol that identifies clients using watermarked data via a paired-subset-difference mechanism, estimating individual client updates from differenced secure aggregation queries, scoring them, and combining scores. Theoretically, FedAttr provides an unbiased client update estimator with bounded privacy leakage. Empirically, it achieves 100% TPR and 0% FPR, significantly outperforming baselines with only 6.3% overhead.

PairAlign: A Framework for Sequence Tokenization via Self-Alignment with Applications to Audio Tokenization

arXiv

Adhiraj Banerjee, Vipul Arora

AI Summary

Existing audio tokenization methods struggle to optimize sequence-level properties like consistency, compactness, and edit similarity due to their local assignment. PairAlign is introduced as a sequence-level self-alignment framework that treats tokenization as conditional sequence generation. It trains an encoder-decoder by making token sequences from one audio view likely under another's representation, using contrastive learning and specific refinements for length control. arXiv

arXiv

AI Summary
