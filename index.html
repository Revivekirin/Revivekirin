EVPeriscope: Extended Perception across Aerial and Ground Vehicles with Event-based Propeller Tracking

arXiv

Dexter Ong, Vijay Kumar, Pratik Chaudhari

AI Summary

Reliable relative localization between aerial and ground robots is difficult for coordinated teams due to conventional camera limitations, motion blur, and environmental factors like occlusion. EVPeriscope proposes an event-based perception system where an upward-facing event camera on a ground robot tracks a quadrotor by detecting its propellers' high-frequency visual signature. This method enables robust localization and closed-loop navigation through challenging conditions, including dense foliage, wind, and varying lighting. The system effectively extends the ground robot's perception where its onboard sensors are degraded or occluded, with quadrotor control operating at 200 Hz.

Near-Optimal Reinforcement Learning with Multi-Step Transition Lookahead

arXiv

Corentin Pla, Hugo Richard, Marc Abeille, Vianney Perchet

AI Summary

Reinforcement learning with multi-step transition lookahead faces NP-hardness for optimal planning, with open questions regarding its generality across discount factors and the feasibility of *near-optimal* efficient planning. The authors propose a randomized polynomial-time approximation scheme for fixed lookahead depths, extended to unknown transitions using optimism and variance-adaptive confidence bounds. They prove exact planning remains NP-hard for any fixed rational discount factor, but their learning algorithm achieves near-optimal cumulative regret matching classical tabular RL, demonstrating efficient near-optimal planning and learning are possible.

arXiv

Jordi Luque, Fernando López, Aleix Sant

AI Summary

arXiv

Fabio Di Pumpo, Tobias Asano, Alexander Friedrich, Gil Paz, Enno Giese

AI Summary

Quantum field theory for fermions on curved or non-inertial backgrounds introduces background and foliation dependencies into canonical structures like the Lagrangian, inner product, and anti-commutation relations, deviating from their standard-Minkowski forms. This work proposes a local fermion-field redefinition, involving rescaling and fixing the local Lorentz frame, to transform the governing generalized temporal gamma matrix. The explicit background dependence is transferred to the transformed Lagrangian and field operators, resulting in a generalized first-order Schrödinger form that includes familiar rest-energy and spacetime-magnetic couplings.

Why Does Post-Training Quantization Work?

arXiv

Yuxiang Chen, Michael Beyer, Jun Zhu, Jianfei Chen

AI Summary

This paper investigates why post-training quantization succeeds in large language models without degrading performance, despite the theoretical expectation that layer-wise quantization errors should compound with depth. By comparing full-precision and quantized forward passes, the authors discover that pretrained models develop counteracting residual interactions, where newly introduced layer errors partially cancel inherited input errors. Additionally, they show that the language model head's geometry preferentially preserves the probabilities of high-ranked tokens, effectively mitigating output disruption across diverse models and quantization settings.
