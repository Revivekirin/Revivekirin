Symplectic Hopf Insulator: Delicate Topology in Bosonic Bogoliubov-de Gennes Systems

arXiv

Isaac Tesfaye, Giandomenico Palumbo

AI Summary

This work constructs a symplectic Hopf insulator in bosonic Bogoliubov-de Gennes (BBdG) systems, realizing a novel topological phase beyond the tenfold-way classification. The proposed method generalizes a Bose-Hubbard model with weak interactions to develop a BBdG system admitting an integer-quantized symplectic Hopf invariant. Key results demonstrate this topology is intrinsically delicate, requiring exactly two bosonic modes, yet robust against weak interactions, and hosts delicate topologically protected in-gap surface states.

arXiv

AI Summary

Learning with Covariance Matrices: Principal Component Analysis Meets Learning with Graphs

arXiv

Saurabh Sihag, Andrea Cavallo, Elvin Isufi, Gonzalo Mateos, Alejandro Ribeiro

AI Summary

Existing graph neural network (GNN) theory inadequately addresses the data-driven nuances of ubiquitous covariance matrices. This work provides theoretical foundations for coVariance Neural Networks (VNNs), which are GNNs specifically designed to operate on covariance matrices as graphs. Key theoretical insights reveal a conceptual equivalence between VNNs and Principal Component Analysis (PCA), refined stability bounds against finite sample perturbations, and improved characterization of VNN transferability. These findings provide justification for adopting VNNs over PCA-based pipelines in applications leveraging covariance matrices.

arXiv

AI Summary

Field quantization in rotating frames: coordinate covariance and the circular-detector response

arXiv

Sidney Natzuka Junior, Carlos Augusto Domingues Zarro, Matheus dos Santos Soares

AI Summary

This paper clarifies scalar-field quantization and detector response in relativistic rotating frames, addressing common misconceptions regarding quantum state representation, global ground state selection, and vacuum response. The authors analyze rigid and Trocheris-Takeno rotating modes, their Bogoliubov coefficients, and the response of a circular Unruh-DeWitt detector in the Minkowski vacuum, utilizing Hadamard subtraction. They demonstrate that rotating modes exhibit no Bogoliubov mixing with inertial modes, and neither rigid nor TT time evolution selects a global ground state. Despite this, a rotating detector shows a stationary, non-thermal excitation in the vacuum, attributed to negative corotating frequencies, proving consistency among vanishing mixing, no rotating vacuum, and detector excitation.

arXiv

AI Summary

Existing vision token pruning methods for multimodal large language models (MLLMs) apply a uniform, one-size-fits-all approach, failing to exploit sample-wise complementarity where different pruning strategies excel on different inputs. To address this, the authors propose VIP-Router, an ultra-lightweight, plug-and-play module that uses low-cost multimodal features to dynamically assign each sample its optimal pruning strategy or retain full tokens when pruning degrades quality.
