CoCo-IR: Contextual Composed Image Retrieval

arXiv

Shengcao Cao, Tanmaya Shekhar Dabral, Zhongli Ding, Madhuri Shanbhogue, Kaifeng Chen, Zhe Li, Mojtaba Seyedhosseini, Yu-Xiong Wang, Liang-Yan Gui

AI Summary

Existing image retrieval systems are limited to single-turn interactions, failing to capture the iterative nature of complex visual searches. The authors introduce Contextual Composed Image Retrieval (CoCo-IR), a multi-turn task, addressed by an LMM-based model that interprets interaction history to generate evolving Transformable Image Embeddings (TIE), trained using a scalable LMM-driven data engine. This approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on the CIRCO benchmark (39.4 mAP@5) and significantly outperforms existing methods on CoCo-IR, demonstrating robust multi-turn performance with 44.1 R@1 on 4-turn dialogues (vs. 28.2).

Argus: A General-Purpose Agentic Runtime for Long-Horizon Reasoning

arXiv

Boxiu Li, Zimo Wen, Yijia Fan, Junxiang Lei, Sufeng Guo, Jiaao Wu, Ruize Tang, Mukai Li, Yifei Shen, Xiaoyu Chen, Wanbo Zhang, Runjing Gu, Yifei Gao, Yuheng Wu, Xuyao Huang, Zelong Zhao, Jiachen Zhang, Shibo Hu, Hangxi Guo, Yilin Chen, Yuzhe Zhang, Fan Yang, Chuan Wen, Xian Zhang, Xuanhe Zhou, Zhijie Deng

AI Summary

Long-horizon reasoning requires an agentic runtime that can adapt to failures and dynamically evolve its approach. Argus is a persistent, self-evolving runtime featuring Manager, Planner, Engineer, and Reviewer roles, which modifies its control policy through verification-gated accumulation of memories, skills, and procedures, without altering model weights. Argus achieves 78% on SWE-Bench Pro (vs. 59% for Direct Copilot) and shows improved token efficiency and workflow time after self-evolution, demonstrating its capability to revise, recover, and accumulate verified approaches across various complex tasks.

Toward Skill-Native LLMs: Skill Entropy for Benchmarking and Training Long-Horizon Reasoning

arXiv

Yinghui He, Ling Yang, Jiarui Liu, Yongjin Yang, Lechen Zhang, Yingcheng Wu, Zhenfei Yin, Mengdi Wang, Sanjeev Arora

AI Summary

The core problem is that large language models (LLMs) struggle with long-horizon reasoning tasks requiring dynamic switching between different skills, which existing benchmarks fail to adequately measure. To address this, the authors introduce "Skill Entropy" as a metric for skill-switching difficulty and propose "Skill^2-Bench," a benchmark of cross-skill long-horizon tasks. They also develop "Skill-Entropy RL," a reinforcement learning framework that uses skill entropy as a training signal to align the model's predicted skill sequence with the gold sequence. Key results show a significant "skill-switching gap" in frontier LLMs, with accuracy decreasing on higher-entropy tasks, and Skill-Entropy RL substantially improves performance on Skill^2-Bench for tested models, indicating its effectiveness as a reusable training signal.

SmartMage: Dynamic Modality Orchestration for 3D Scene Understanding

arXiv

Yue Zhang, Yingzhao Jian, Yunqiu Xu, Xiaoxiao Sun, Hehe Fan

AI Summary

Existing MLLMs for 3D scene understanding suffer from fixed modality combinations, leading to semantic noise and inefficient reasoning as modality relevance varies per query. SmartMage is proposed as a unified MLLM that dynamically orchestrates heterogeneous modalities. It uses a Semantic-guided Modality Adaptive RouTng (SMART) module for selecting task-relevant modalities and a Modality-Aware Gating Expert (MAGE) module for adaptive expert activation. SmartMage achieves state-of-the-art performance across five 3D scene understanding benchmarks and competitive results on RGB-only video understanding, with diagnostic analysis validating its semantic-aware modality orchestration.

OPD-V: Visual On-Policy Self-Distillation with Modality Balance

arXiv

Aniri, Jinhe Bi, Peng Liao, Zengjie Jin, Volker Tresp, Fei Shen, Yunpu Ma, Tat-Seng Chua

AI Summary

On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD) for MLLMs is limited by Modality Imbalance, where dominant textual information prevents full integration of multimodal input and underutilizes privileged information. To address this, OPD-V introduces a visual OPSD paradigm that leverages Modality Balance itself as privileged information. It uses Positive (Zoom-In) and Negative (Mask) Teachers to define Modality-Balance Logits Margins, establishing a Modality-Balance Trust Region for selecting on-policy tokens for distillation. OPD-V consistently improves reasoning performance across diverse benchmarks and MLLM backbones while reducing training cost.

SSTQ:Privacy-Preserving Vector Quantization via Subsampled Stochastic TurboQuant

arXiv

Adel Javanmard, David P. Woodruff, Vahab Mirrokni

AI Summary

The core problem is achieving local differential privacy and low communication cost in distributed optimization while avoiding unfavorable variance from existing vector quantization methods. This paper proposes Subsampled Stochastic TurboQuant (SSTQ), a framework combining overcomplete tight frames, coordinate subsampling, and privacy-aware one-dimensional quantization, with two variants. SSTQ achieves optimal mean squared error scaling with low communication bits and a new objective reduces codebook-dependent MSE scaling from $O(4^b)$ to $O(2^b)$. Empirical results demonstrate SSTQ's favorable utility and communication efficiency in federated learning tasks.

Reward Structure Shapes the Interaction Between Episodic Exploration and Neural Memory in Reinforcement Learning

arXiv

Jai Malegaonkar, Rohan Patil, Henrik I. Christensen

AI Summary

This paper investigates the interaction between episodic exploration bonuses and neural memory architectures in partially observable reinforcement learning, addressing ambiguities in sparse reward definitions. The authors conduct a controlled study crossing exploration bonuses with diverse memory architectures across environments varying memory content acquisition, and formalize reward sparsity. Key results demonstrate that a bonus's impact on architectural capacity differences is highly dependent on the reward structure and how memory content is acquired. Ultimately, exploration and memory are found to be complements, with bonuses inducing exposure and memory converting that exposure into effective returns.

arXiv

Dibyajyoti Chakraborty, Romit Maulik

AI Summary

This study introduces a unified approach to atmospheric data assimilation (DA) to flexibly integrate multimodal spatiotemporal data and perform various DA tasks. ABSeeker: Training Long-Horizon Search Agents via Answer-Backtracked Credit Assignment

arXiv

Yijun Lu, Rui Ye, Jiajun Wang, Yuwen Du, Tian Jin, Songhua Liu, Siheng Chen

AI Summary

Existing methods for training long-horizon search agents struggle due to uniform credit assignment across all trajectory steps, failing to distinguish useful actions from errors. ABSeeker proposes Answer-Backtracked Credit Assignment (ABC), a fine-grained framework that converts sparse trajectory outcomes into dense step-level supervision by tracing back from the ground-truth answer to recover intermediate clues and scoring each step against them. Implementing ABC via ABC-SFT and ABC-GRPO, the 4B ABSeeker achieves 55.3% on BrowseComp and 52.9% on BrowseComp-ZH (with context management), significantly outperforming same-scale agents and matching the performance of much larger 30B models.

Learning When to Stop: Prefix-Optimal Dynamic Diffusion Policies for Continuous Control

arXiv

Rohit Kumar Salla, Manoj Saravanan, Simon Stepputtis

AI Summary

Diffusion policies for continuous control face a substantial computational bottleneck from iterative denoising, requiring adaptive step reduction without sacrificing performance. POGP addresses this by learning a prefix value function at each intermediate denoising step via a Bellman-style recursion, serving as both an auxiliary training objective and a test-time early stopping rule. This approach reduces denoising iterations by approximately 2.7-fold with near-full task performance and improves final task performance by 3.5% compared to state-of-the-art dynamic diffusion baselines.

arXiv

AI Summary

arXiv

AI Summary

Exact Model-Free Policy Iteration for Co-safe LTL Planning

arXiv

Zetong Xuan, Yu Wang

AI Summary

Direct model-free RL for co-safe LTL objectives struggles to converge to optimal policies due to noncontractive Bellman equations and nonunique solutions. This work proposes a two-step exact model-free policy iteration method: it first uses a discounted surrogate to identify a "clamp set" to resolve nonuniqueness, then performs undiscounted policy evaluation and greedy improvement. The method is proven to converge almost-surely in policy evaluation and terminate finitely at an optimal policy, validated through experiments.

Scaling behavior in non-reciprocal and odd conserved dynamics near criticality

arXiv

Martin Kjøllesdal Johnsrud, Giulia Pisegna, Ramin Golestanian

AI Summary

This study investigates the scaling behavior of the non-reciprocal Cahn-Hilliard (NRCH) model near criticality, which describes active matter systems with non-reciprocal interactions and non-equilibrium activity. Using perturbative dynamical renormalization group techniques, the authors analyze structural and dynamical correlations. They found that these correlations are controlled by different correlation lengths, both diverging at criticality but with distinct scaling laws. While structural correlations follow classical scaling, dynamical correlations exhibit multiple scaling regimes, dominated by either temperature or non-reciprocal coupling, characterized by a new critical exponent.

Optimal Constrained sc-LTL Planning in MDPs via Switching Policies

arXiv

Zetong Xuan, Yu Wang

AI Summary

This paper addresses optimal policy synthesis in Markov Decision Processes (MDPs) with non-Markovian co-safe LTL (sc-LTL) objectives and safety constraints. The proposed method reduces this problem to constrained reachability on an extended model, showing that a class of switching policies, constructed from stationary policies, is sufficient for optimality. This allows optimal policies to be computed via a tractable linear program, demonstrated by a grid world case study achieving an optimal trade-off between objective and safety.
