CustomDancer: Customized Dance Recommendation by Text-Dance Retrieval

arXiv

Yawen Qin, Ke Qiu, Qin Zhang

AI Summary

Personalized dance discovery is challenging due to the complexity of text-dance retrieval, which requires integrating linguistic semantics, musical rhythm, and full-body motion. To address this, the authors introduce TD-Data, a large-scale dataset for text-dance retrieval, and propose CustomDancer, a multimodal framework that aligns text with dance using a CLIP-based text encoder, music and motion encoders, and a music-motion blending module. CustomDancer achieves state-of-the-art performance on TD-Data, reaching 10.23% Recall@1 and showing improved retrieval quality in both quantitative and user preference studies.

Persistent Visual Memory: Sustaining Perception for Deep Generation in LVLMs

arXiv

Siyuan Huang, Xiaoye Qu, Yafu Li, Tong Zhu, Zefeng He, Muxin Fu, Daizong Liu, Wei-Long Zheng, Yu Cheng

AI Summary

Autoregressive LVLMs face "Visual Signal Dilution," where visual attention decays with increasing textual history due to an expanding attention partition function. To counter this, the paper proposes Persistent Visual Memory (PVM), a lightweight learnable module integrated as a parallel branch to provide a distance-agnostic retrieval pathway for sustained visual embeddings. PVM demonstrates significant accuracy gains on Qwen3-VL models with negligible overhead, especially in complex reasoning, by resisting length-induced signal decay and accelerating prediction convergence.

arXiv

Stavros Orfanoudakis, Pedro P. Vergara

AI Summary

Reinforcement Learning rarely uses representation and similarity learning to directly shape policy updates in the action space. SAVGO proposes learning a joint state-action embedding space where cosine similarity reflects action-value estimates, which then generates a similarity kernel to guide policy improvement towards higher-value regions. The structure of gauge invariant Gaussian quantum operations on finite Fermion systems

arXiv

Eric A. Carlen

AI Summary

The core problem is to characterize gauge invariant Gaussian quantum operations and their semigroups on finite Fermion systems that preserve gauge invariant Gaussian states (${\mathfrak S}_{GIG}$). The proposed method involves first deriving a structure theorem for such individual quantum operations on the gauge-fixed subalgebra (${\mathscr G}_{{\mathcal H}_1}$). Key results show that semigroups of these operations are parameterized by pairs $(G,A)$, where $G$ is a contraction semigroup generator and $0 \leq A \leq -G -G^*$. These semigroups also possess a natural extension to the full CAR algebra.

EASE: Federated Multimodal Unlearning via Entanglement-Aware Anchor Closure

arXiv

Zihao Ding, Beining Wu, Jun Huang

AI Summary

Federated multimodal unlearning is challenged by entangled forgotten knowledge across modalities and client gradient subspaces, with forgotten alignments persisting through cross-modal coupling, subspace entanglement, and federated updates. The proposed EASE framework addresses this by bilaterally displacing visual and language branches to close cross-modal reconstruction, employing Cosine--Sine decomposition to isolate forget-exclusive update directions, and using a direction-selective Forget Lock to bound residual drift. STARE: Step-wise Temporal Alignment and Red-teaming Engine for Multi-modal Toxicity Attack

arXiv

Xutao Mao, Liangjie Zhao, Tao Liu, Xiang Zheng, Hongying Zan, Cong Wang

AI Summary

Existing red-teaming for VLM toxicity treats image generation as a black box, failing to identify when and how toxic semantics emerge during multi-step synthesis. The proposed method, STARE, is a hierarchical reinforcement learning framework that treats the entire denoising trajectory as the attack surface, coupling a high-level prompt editor with low-level T2I fine-tuning via Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO). Augmented Lagrangian Multiplier Network for State-wise Safety in Reinforcement Learning

arXiv

Jiaming Zhang, Yujie Yang, Yao Lyu, Shengbo Eben Li, Liping Zhang

AI Summary

Standard dual gradient ascent for state-wise safety constraints, when approximated by multiplier networks, suffers from severe training oscillations due to network generalization, which existing scalar stabilization techniques cannot address. The proposed Augmented Lagrangian Multiplier Network (ALaM) framework mitigates this by introducing a quadratic penalty for local convexity and training the multiplier network via supervised regression toward a dual target. ALaM theoretically guarantees multiplier convergence and optimal policy recovery. Empirically, the SAC-ALaM algorithm outperforms state-of-the-art safe RL baselines in safety, return, and training stability, learning well-calibrated risk identification multipliers.

Affordance Agent Harness: Verification-Gated Skill Orchestration

arXiv

Haojian Huang, Jiahao Shi, Yinchuan Li, Yingcong Chen

AI Summary

Affordance grounding in open-world scenes is challenged by fixed multi-skill pipelines that poorly adapt to instance difficulty, lack targeted error recovery, and fail to reuse experience, leading to unreliable predictions under inference costs. The proposed Affordance Agent Harness (AAH) is a closed-loop runtime that unifies heterogeneous skills via an evidence store and cost control, incorporating episodic memory for recurring objects. AAH adaptively selects and parameterizes skills with a Router, while an affordance-specific Verifier gates commitments using self-consistency and evidence sufficiency, triggering targeted retries. This approach achieves a stronger accuracy-cost Pareto frontier than fixed-pipeline baselines, improving grounding quality while reducing average skill calls and latency.

Budget Constraints as Riemannian Manifolds

arXiv

Michael Helcig, Dan Alistarh

AI Summary

The paper addresses optimizing non-decomposable objectives for K-option assignments under strict total cost budgets, a problem where existing methods either rely on proxies, lack gradients, or enforce constraints approximately with sensitive hyperparameters. It proposes Riemannian Constrained Optimization (RCO), which observes that softmax-relaxed budget constraints define a simple Riemannian manifold in logit space, allowing first-order optimization via Adam augmented with tangent projection, binary-search retraction, and momentum transport, combined with Gumbel straight-through for exact budget enforcement. RCO recovers optimal solutions on synthetic problems, outperforming penalty methods, and matches or exceeds evolutionary search on LLM compression tasks (e.g., quantization, pruning) while requiring 3x-16x lower wall-clock cost.
