arXiv

Dengyang Jiang, Xin Jin, Dongyang Liu, Zanyi Wang, Mingzhe Zheng, Ruoyi Du, Xiangpeng Yang, Qilong Wu, Zhen Li, Peng Gao, Harry Yang, Steven Hoi

AI Summary

Continuously fine-tuning efficient few-step diffusion models using standard methods compromises their inherent rapid inference capability. D-OPSD proposes an on-policy self-distillation paradigm where the model acts as both a text-conditioned student and a multimodal-conditioned teacher, optimizing predicted distributions over the student's roll-outs. A Closed-Form Dual-Barrier CBF Safety Filter for Holonomic Robots on Incrementally Built Occupancy Grid Maps

ACL

Himanshu Paudel, Basanta Joshi, Dhirendra Raj Madai, Alina Bartaula, Biman Rimal, Sanjay Neupane

AI Summary

This paper tackles the core problem of ensuring real-time collision avoidance for holonomic robots in unknown, incrementally mapped environments, specifically addressing collision risk from uncertain, unexplored regions. It proposes a closed-form dual-barrier Control Barrier Function (CBF) safety filter that enforces two analytical constraints: avoidance of mapped obstacles and restriction from unexplored regions, derived from the occupancy grid's signed distance field. The filter employs an adaptive gain schedule for exploration efficiency, has low computational overhead, and minimally corrects nominal velocities. Hardware flight experiments on a quadrotor demonstrated zero collisions across multiple indoor runs.

Dynamical correlations in a dissipative XXZ spin chain

arXiv

Cătălin Paşcu Moca, Doru Sticlet, Ovidiu I. Pâţu, Balazs Dóra

AI Summary

This paper investigates dynamical spin correlations in a dissipative XXZ spin chain with uniform local spin-loss and pumping, starting from a mixed steady state. The study employs vectorized TEBD for numerical simulations and exact analytical results via Pfaffian representation and third-quantization for the XX case. For unitary evolution, longitudinal correlators exhibit established spin-transport universality classes (ballistic, KPZ, diffusive) with superimposed magnon-induced ballistic propagation, while transverse correlators show diverse scaling depending on the regime. Under full Lindbladian dynamics, these universality classes are preserved at early times but acquire an overall exponential damping in the long-time limit.

Quantum Entanglement in the Dirac Field Quantization around Charged Black Holes

arXiv

Abdessamie Chhieb, Chaimae Banouni, Saliha Abdessamie, Mohamed Ouchrif

AI Summary

This paper investigates Dirac field quantum entanglement near charged Reissner-Nordström black holes, incorporating Hawking radiation effects. Entanglement is quantified using concurrence and Bures distance, analyzing its evolution with black hole charge (Q), mode frequency (ω), and initial entanglement angle (θ). Key findings show that Q enhances decoherence inside the horizon while temporarily increasing accessible entanglement outside, and the Hawking effect causes apparent entanglement loss for external observers due to correlation transfer. High-frequency modes exhibit greater resilience to gravitational effects, maintaining robust correlations near the horizon.

Graph-SND: Sparse Aggregation for Behavioral Diversity in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

arXiv

Shawn Ray

AI Summary

System Neural Diversity (SND) measures multi-agent behavioral heterogeneity but incurs quadratic computational cost by averaging all pairwise agent distances. Graph-SND is introduced, replacing this complete-graph average with a weighted average over a sparse graph's edges or using random edge sampling, reducing complexity to $O(|E|)$ while providing an unbiased estimator. The method proves theoretical bounds for various graph structures and empirically tracks full SND accurately, reducing computation time by 10x or more and maintaining performance in both passive measurement and closed-loop diversity control.
