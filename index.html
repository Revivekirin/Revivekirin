Diagnosing Compositional Generalization in Sequential Robot Tasks

arXiv

Yixiao Wang, Cheng-En Wu, Lingfeng Sun, Pengcheng Wang, Xiang Ji, Boyuan Liang, Guojian Zhan, Masayoshi Tomizuka

AI Summary

Robot policies struggle with compositional generalization on novel instruction combinations due to the combinatorial expense of exhaustive data collection. This paper diagnoses the generalization gap by decomposing it into marginal, compositional, and context-action shifts, analyzing instruction-space coverage. Results show that exhaustive data is unnecessary; a structured subset (25% of tasks) covering action-relevant dependencies recovers strong out-of-distribution performance. Critically, sparse training failures often stem from instruction steering rather than missing low-level skills, emphasizing the importance of capturing relational structure and dependency coverage for efficient data collection.

Differentially Private Nonparametric Modal Learning with Applications to Regression and Clustering

arXiv

Arkajyoti Bhattacharjee, Arnab Auddy

AI Summary

This paper addresses the largely unexplored problem of estimating density modes for multimodal distributions under rigorous differential privacy constraints. It proposes DP-GRAMS, a mean-shift inspired method that performs noisy ascent on a differentially private score estimator using bias-reducing higher-order kernels, gradient clipping, and calibrated Gaussian noise, with a private initialization scheme. The method recovers all population modes with high probability, achieving nearly optimal asymptotic error rates and providing minimax lower bounds for private mode estimation. Extensions for private modal-regression (DP-PMS) and clustering (DP-GRAMS-C) are presented, demonstrating favorable privacy-utility trade-offs.

GQ-FSL: Green Quantized Federated Split Learning

arXiv

Idan Roth, Lutz Lampe

AI Summary

Deploying DNNs at the wireless edge faces severe energy constraints on mobile devices, even with FSL, due to continuous data and submodel exchanges. The paper proposes Green Quantized FSL (GQ-FSL), which incorporates stochastic quantization for training and transmissions, allowing asymmetric precision levels for client and server submodels. GQ-FSL formulates a joint optimization problem to configure DNN split points and precision, minimizing system energy consumption under accuracy constraints, supported by energy models and theoretical convergence bounds. Key results demonstrate GQ-FSL enables large-scale DNN deployment on resource-constrained devices with superior energy efficiency compared to quantized FL and full-precision FSL.

arXiv

AI Summary

The core problem is the extensive setup and training time required for error-controlled classification systems in flexible manufacturing, hindering rapid deployment. arXiv

Wenxin Tang, Jingyu Xiao, Zhenyu Liu, Zipeng Xie, Junliang Liu, Wang Luo, Yuan Jiang, Yintong Huo, Michael Lyu

AI Summary

Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) processing source code images face high input costs due to inefficient blank regions and task-irrelevant content, inadequately addressed by fixed resolution scaling. CodeShrink, an adaptive visual compression framework, is proposed with three components: Blank-Free Rendering for compact layouts, an RL agent for per-input configuration, and Dominant Token Selection for instruction-aware pruning. arXiv

AI Summary

Visual imitation learning policies are brittle to camera perturbations because RGB observations lack explicit geometric cues. To address this, RayViT injects camera geometry, represented as a Plücker ray map, into pretrained ViT backbones using gated cross-attention to create a geometry-aware class token and dense positional embeddings. When Does On-Policy Interaction Help? Representational Tradeoffs in Value-Based Imitation Learning

ACL

Luca Viano, Antoine Moulin, Audrey Huang, Volkan Cevher, Philip Amortila, Dylan J. Foster

AI Summary

Imitation learning (IL) faces compounding errors when the learner cannot perfectly represent the expert's policy, a problem traditionally addressed by interactive learning or value function estimation. This work proposes OVI, an interactive on-policy IL algorithm, based on the finding that expert interaction relaxes representational demands, requiring only the ability to realize the expert's value function rather than their full policy. OVI is statistically and computationally efficient under expert-value realizability, a benefit proven to necessitate interaction for this relaxed demand. Empirically, OVI outperforms various offline and interactive IL methods, particularly when the learner network is less expressive than the expert.

arXiv

AI Summary

HAM-VLN: Harnessing Hierarchical Agentic Memory for Zero-Shot Vision-and-Language Navigation

arXiv

An Liu, Bingxi Liu, Hongyu Ding, Yixuan Jiang, Yaran Chen, Fulin Tang, Cong Leng, Hong Zhang, Jian Cheng

AI Summary

Long-horizon Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN) using training-free multimodal LLMs suffers from a growing memory and reasoning bottleneck due to increasing context length. HAM-VLN proposes a decision-coupled, agent-authored hierarchical memory system: a persistent, depth-grounded world graph that records semantic and reflective information, retaining recent waypoints verbatim while retrieving older history based on relevance, recency, and salience. This approach integrates memory updates into the per-waypoint decision LLM call, requiring no extra calls. Convergence and Regret of the Policy Gradient for Multi-Armed Bandits in Diffusion Environment

arXiv

Yanwei Jia, Du Ouyang

AI Summary

This paper analyzes the policy gradient update for a multi-arm bandit problem in a continuous-time diffusion environment modeled by a stochastic differential equation. Using logit parameterization for the stochastic policy, the authors propose a novel Lyapunov function to rigorously analyze its behavior. arXiv

Kasra Sinaei, Hung-Chieh Wu, Donald Ebeigbe

AI Summary

Existing methods for safe Vision Language Action models rely on external safety filters that can alter output intent. This paper proposes a modular inference framework that integrates Flow Matching generative models with formal Control Barrier Functions (CBF) by modifying the Flow Matching denoising process using a smooth Log-Sum-Exponential aggregate barrier. Edge asymptotics for weighted Robin equations on cylinders with applications to spectral fractional Laplacians

arXiv

Alessandra De Luca, Veronica Felli, Stefano Vita

AI Summary

This paper addresses the problem of deriving asymptotic estimates of solutions near the edge of cylinders for weighted degenerate or singular problems with Robin boundary conditions. The proposed method employs Almgren-type monotonicity techniques. Key results include obtaining local asymptotics and unique continuation from the boundary for both Robin and Neumann spectral fractional Laplacians. The local decay rates for these solutions are found to be identical for both Neumann and Robin problems, explicitly quantized by a weighted spherical spectral problem.
